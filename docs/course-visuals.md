# Visuel du cours court métrage IA

Outil : ImageGen intégré. Fichier final : `apps/main/public/media/film-course-mariama.png`.
Illustration pédagogique générée par IA du personnage fictif Mariama ; aucune
personne de l’équipe ou cliente réelle n’est représentée. Le visuel est utilisé
pour le programme public et les expériences de cadrage et de couleur. Les schémas
de continuité et les timelines sont construits en SVG et CSS.

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: educational cinematic still for Smartsell Academy's adult course Faire un court-métrage avec l'IA. Subject: Mariama, a fictional West African Guinean adult seamstress age 34, quiet determined thoughtful expression, modest indigo clothing, seated in her small Conakry courtyard at dawn. A covered sewing machine and hanging fabrics are recognizable background landmarks. Camera at eye level, medium-wide framing; adult woman's full head and upper body centered within the middle vertical third so learners can experiment with 16:9 to 9:16 crops. Warm early sunlight from screen left, subtle cinematic grain, natural deep skin tones, indigo shadows and amber highlights, sophisticated understated cinematic composition. Clearly fictional illustrative scene, not a real customer or team member. No children, no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no split panels, no fake user interface. High quality photographic cinematic still. Keep the central face well lit and unobscured, generous background space on both sides.

Outil : ImageGen intégré. Fichier final : `apps/main/public/media/academy-guide.png`.
Avatar fictif d’une formatrice, signalé comme illustration IA dans l’interface.

l’apprenant. Ce guide présente les explications du cours et leurs corrections
prédéfinies ; il ne simule pas une présence humaine en direct.

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait of a fictional virtual instructor for Smartsell Academy's interactive guided AI filmmaking course. A warm, confident adult West African Guinean female educator around 38, looking into camera with a welcoming engaged expression, modest elegant deep purple jacket over cream top. Medium close portrait, shoulders and head fully visible, hands out of frame. Cinematic editorial 3D illustration with refined natural facial proportions, subtle stylisation, soft realistic skin textures, sophisticated warm lighting. Minimal deep violet studio backdrop with a soft yellow rim glow. Portrait composition, no text, no logos, no UI, no whiteboard text. Clearly an invented educational avatar, not a photograph of a real instructor or team member. Friendly and professional, not childish, not sci-fi robot.
